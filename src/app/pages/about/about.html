Fahrel Ardhan Assegaff

Full-Stack & Cross-Platform Software Engineer

Web, mobile, POS & enterprise systems · Open to full-time roles & freelance

What keeps me hooked on engineering is taking messy, real-world operational bottlenecks and turning them into clean software that actually moves the needle — not over-engineered demos built to look good on a whiteboard.

Over 4+ years, I’ve shipped production web and mobile apps across very different arenas: warehouse SKU inventory portals, retail e-commerce and POS systems used under real store pressure, enterprise procurement workflows, BCA branch queuing maintenance, reseller scrapers, and a national university admission portal serving thousands of concurrent applicants.

I prefer owning the full path: database schemas that don’t crack under scale, secure REST APIs, and interfaces that stay responsive even on spotty mobile connections. Little details matter.
