ABOUT ME

Engineering Journey & Technical Mindset

What keeps me hooked on software engineering is genuinely wanting to know how modern tech can untangle messy, real-world operational problems and actually turn into business value, not just theory on a whiteboard.

Over 4+ years, I've built and shipped production-grade web and mobile apps across some pretty different worlds: enterprise inventory systems (SKU management), retail eCommerce/POS platforms that need to work in real stores under real pressure, and even a national university admission portal used by thousands of people at once.

I like working across the whole stack. That means designing database schemas that won't fall apart under scale, integrating RESTful APIs securely, and building interfaces that feel smooth whether someone's on a laptop or a phone with spotty signal. Most of this happens inside Agile Scrum teams, so communication and adapting on the fly are just as much a part of the job as the code itself.

At the end of the day, what drives me is building software that's stable, scalable, and easy to maintain long after I've moved on to the next sprint.
